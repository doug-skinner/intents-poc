# Apr 28, 2026

I strongly believe that the future of product work tracking doesn't look anything like the github issues/jira/linear systems that we have today. Those systems were built for a world where execution was expensive, so the largest constraint that needed to be managed was developer time and focus. You needed to allow developers to stay focused on their work, focused on the way the product should be built, and reduce the overhead of what needed to be done. At the same time, you needed accountability for what was going to be built, and when.

This lead to systems like Jira, where everything tried to be accounted for. And because it's impossible to make a single system that kept everyone happy, you tended to have things like engineering focused work tracked in github, product focused work tracked in jira, and some weird process trying to relate the two. Another thing that this world had a hard time dealing with was the appropriate linking between engineering work and what was supposed to happen with a product. We've all been there, opening a jira issue to fix a quick typo in a readme and wondering how on earth we got here. It came from a good place, or a regulatory required place, but I think we can improve it. These issues have gotten more important to solve over the last few years as the pace of development has increased, and have become existential to solve in the age of coding agents where if you had the money to burn execution can now happen 24x7.

I believe the new future is intent declaration and tracking. We need to step back from trying to rigidly control exactly what happens in our products, in our work tracking systems, and in our code bases. Product owners should no longer have to care what the engineering work tracking looks like, nor that everything is linked up appropriately.

This repo is my place to explore this idea, and see how it evolves.
